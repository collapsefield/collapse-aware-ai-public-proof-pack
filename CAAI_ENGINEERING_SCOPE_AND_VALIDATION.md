# CAAI Engineering Scope and Validation

## Purpose

This document describes the practical engineering scope of Collapse Aware AI.

## Engineering Focus

Collapse Aware AI is intended to provide:

- memory-weighted behaviour
- continuity support
- behavioural stability
- drift management
- weighted behavioural influence
- model-agnostic integration

## Evaluation

These goals should be evaluated through:

- demonstrations
- benchmarks
- validation reports
- behavioural comparisons
- runtime testing

## Scope Boundary

This document concerns software behaviour and system performance.

It is not a claim of proof regarding broader theoretical, physical, or consciousness-related questions.

## Practical Question

The key question is:

Does the middleware improve continuity and behavioural stability in measurable ways?

That question can be tested directly.
