## Core Declaration

Collapse Aware AI (CAAI) is an original proprietary middleware architecture developed by **Marcos Verrell Moss Ross (M.R.)** under **Inappropriate Media Limited (t/a Collapse Aware AI)**.

CAAI is designed to operate as a behavioural control layer around existing models, scripted systems, game AI, simulation agents, or agent frameworks.

It does not require training a new foundation model and does not require modifying base model weights.

The original contribution is the middleware architecture for governed, memory-weighted behavioural continuity.

---

## What Makes the Architecture Distinct

CAAI combines the following public architectural principles:

### Memory-Weighted Behaviour

Past events, retained moments, and anchors can influence future behavioural selection through structured weighting.

### Governor-Controlled Drift Prevention

A Governor layer constrains unstable behaviour, contradiction, over-improvisation, repetition loops, and loss of continuity.

### Contract-First Integration

CAAI is designed to integrate through stable interfaces and adapters without exposing proprietary core logic.

### Collapse Selection

Candidate behaviours can be scored, weighted, constrained, and selected rather than accepted directly from a base model or host runtime.

### Public / Private Boundary

The public architecture can be described, reviewed, and cited while the sealed Crown implementation remains proprietary.

## Relationship to Existing AI and Agent Systems

CAAI may be used with existing LLMs, game AI systems, scripted logic, simulation agents, or workflow agents.

That does not make the CAAI middleware architecture a derivative of those systems.

The correct distinction is:

> Existing systems may generate candidate behaviour.  
> Collapse Aware AI governs, weights, and selects behaviour through a proprietary middleware control layer.

This document does not claim that CAAI invented LLMs, transformers, agent frameworks, or traditional game AI.

It claims authorship over the Collapse Aware AI architecture and its specific combination of memory-weighted selection, Governor logic, continuity memory, anchors, and contract-first behavioural control.

## Relationship to Verrell’s Law

CAAI is the applied engineering branch associated with Verrell’s Law.

Verrell’s Law provides the broader theoretical frame that retained information can bias future state selection.

CAAI implements that principle in software through memory weighting, continuity state, anchors, Governor logic, and controlled behavioural selection.

This should be read as an engineering application, not as a claim that the full scientific or physical form of Verrell’s Law has been experimentally proven.

Public-safe wording:

> Verrell’s Law motivates the architecture. Collapse Aware AI implements the principle as software middleware.
