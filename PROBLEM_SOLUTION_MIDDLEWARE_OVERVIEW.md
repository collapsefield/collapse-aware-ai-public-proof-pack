# Problem / Solution Middleware Overview

## Purpose

This document explains Collapse Aware AI in practical engineering language.

---

## Problem: Characters Forget

Many systems lose continuity when context disappears.

### CAAI Response

Continuity Memory

Retained behavioural moments can influence future behaviour.

---

## Problem: Behaviour Drifts

Characters, agents, or assistants may gradually diverge from intended behaviour.

### CAAI Response

Governor Control

Behaviour can be constrained, stabilised, and routed through governance layers.

---

## Problem: Relationships Reset

Past interactions often have little influence on future interactions.

### CAAI Response

Memory Weighting

Important prior events can remain behaviourally relevant.

---

## Problem: Every Session Feels New

Users often experience discontinuity between sessions.

### CAAI Response

Weighted Emergence Layering (WEL)

Past behavioural collapses are carried forward as weighted influence over future selection.

---

## Problem: AI Feels Inconsistent

Different responses can emerge from very similar circumstances.

### CAAI Response

Anchors + Governor + Continuity

Behaviour can remain more coherent across time.

---

## Public Summary

> Collapse Aware AI is middleware designed to improve continuity, reduce drift, preserve behavioural state, and govern how prior events influence future decisions.

This document describes engineering goals, not proof of broader theoretical claims.
