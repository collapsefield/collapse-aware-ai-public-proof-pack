# Why Not Just Use an LLM?

## Short Answer

Large language models generate candidate text.

Collapse Aware AI governs behavioural continuity.

That is the difference.

---

## LLMs Generate

A base LLM predicts likely next tokens from context.

It can be useful, flexible, and expressive, but it does not automatically provide reliable long-term behavioural continuity, persistent character state, relationship memory, drift control, or governed behavioural selection across sessions.

---

## CAAI Governs Continuity

Collapse Aware AI sits around or above an underlying model, scripted logic layer, or decision engine.

It uses retained behavioural state, memory weighting, anchors, recency, salience, Weighted Emergence Layering, and Governor logic to influence future selection.

The goal is not simply to generate text.

The goal is to make behaviour remain coherent over time.

---

## Practical Difference

| Problem | Plain LLM Risk | CAAI Direction |
|---|---|---|
| NPC forgets prior interaction | Context loss | Continuity memory |
| Character drifts out of role | Probability drift | Governor control |
| Relationship resets | No persistent behavioural state | Weighted anchors |
| Behaviour changes randomly | Unbounded variation | Governed selection |
| Session-to-session inconsistency | Context window limit | Stored state influence |

---

## Clean Public Position

> LLMs generate candidates. CAAI governs which behaviours should remain consistent, weighted, constrained, and continuous over time.

---

## Claim Boundary

This document does not claim that CAAI replaces LLMs.

It clarifies that CAAI is designed to complement models and decision engines by adding governed memory-weighted behavioural continuity.
