# Common Questions For Studios

## Why not just use GPT or another LLM?

LLMs generate outputs.

Collapse Aware AI focuses on continuity, behavioural stability, memory-weighted influence, and drift control.

## Why not just add memory?

Memory storage and behavioural influence are different problems.

CAAI focuses on how remembered events affect future behaviour.

## Does this require retraining a model?

No.

CAAI is designed as middleware around existing systems.

## What problem does it solve?

- continuity loss
- behavioural drift
- relationship reset problems
- inconsistent character behaviour
- weak long-term state persistence

## Is it only for games?

No.

Games are the initial target.

The architecture may also apply to simulations, agents, assistants, and other continuity-sensitive systems.

## Does this prove Verrell's Law?

No.

CAAI is an engineering implementation.

The wider Verrell's Law research programme remains separate.

## What should a studio evaluate?

- continuity improvement
- behavioural stability
- memory influence
- governor effectiveness
- integration simplicity
- runtime performance
