# Public Buyer Review Note

## Purpose

This note is for technical reviewers, licensing partners, studios, agent-framework teams, simulation teams, and enterprise evaluators reviewing Collapse Aware AI from public materials.

Collapse Aware AI should be evaluated as an engineering system.

Verrell’s Law is the wider research framework. It is relevant background, but a buyer does not need to accept every broader research hypothesis in order to evaluate the middleware.

---

## Clean Evaluation Position

Collapse Aware AI is proprietary, engine-agnostic middleware for governed retained-state behavioural selection.

The practical question is:

> does retained state alter future behavioural selection in a controlled, inspectable, and useful way compared with a baseline?

That is the evaluation centre.

---

## What To Evaluate

A serious technical review should focus on:

- structured input / output contract
- retained-state influence
- candidate behaviour selection
- governor-constrained resolution
- persistence and restart behaviour
- fallback handling
- validation boundaries
- diagnostic visibility
- integration feasibility
- baseline comparison

These are software questions.

---

## What Not To Confuse It With

Collapse Aware AI is not presented as:

- a foundation model
- AGI
- machine consciousness
- ordinary memory storage
- a vector database
- a standard RAG layer
- a chatbot skin
- proof of new physics
- proof of the full scientific scope of Verrell’s Law

The middleware can succeed or fail on engineering evidence alone.

---

## Relationship To Verrell’s Law

Verrell’s Law investigates retained information, weighting, observation, memory, and selection across systems.

Collapse Aware AI applies a narrower part of that structure in software:

```text
candidate behaviours
        ↓
retained-state influence
        ↓
governor constraints
        ↓
selected behaviour
        ↓
diagnostic evidence
```

A reviewer may reject speculative physics-facing interpretations of Verrell’s Law and still evaluate CAAI on runtime behaviour.

That separation is deliberate.

---

## Current Public Status

Public materials describe the architecture, evaluation logic, and claim boundaries while protecting proprietary implementation details.

Private implementation details, production tuning, thresholds, kernel logic, handoff packs, private governor configuration, watermarking logic, and commercial integration materials remain reserved.

This public repository is an evaluation and proof-orientation layer, not an open-source release of the commercial implementation.

---

## Commercial Direction

The preferred commercial route is a time-limited architecture evaluation under appropriate confidentiality terms.

The evaluation should answer one practical question:

> does governed retained-state selection provide useful behavioural continuity, control, and inspectability for the target system?

That is the licensing conversation.
